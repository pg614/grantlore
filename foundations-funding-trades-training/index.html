How we built this list

We searched the grant purpose and recipient name of every itemized grant in the latest 990-PF of the foundations on Grantlore for terms such as skilled trades, vocational, apprentice, workforce, job training, career and technical education, welding, plumbing, HVAC, electrician, carpentry, construction training and YouthBuild. A foundation is listed if at least one grant matched. Dollar figures are the sum of matching grants only, as filed. A keyword match isn't proof of a trades focus: open each profile and read the grants before applying. Sorted by application status (accepts applications first), then by matching dollars.

Covers the Grantlore directory only (private foundations meeting our size and grant-pattern criteria). Corporate programs like Lowe's and Home Depot are on the trade training grants tracker.
