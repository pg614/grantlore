Frequently asked questions

Are there grants for individuals to attend trade school?

Yes. As of Oct 29, 2026, options include the mikeroweWORKS Work Ethic Scholarship (final 2026 deadline Oct 31), the Home Depot Foundation Path to Pro $2,500 scholarship (year-round), WIOA Individual Training Accounts through your local American Job Center (OhioMeansJobs in Ohio), and Workforce Pell Grants for approved short programs.

What is Workforce Pell?

Workforce Pell lets eligible students use Pell Grants for approved workforce programs of 150–599 clock hours lasting 8 to under 15 weeks. The federal rule took effect July 20, 2026, with early use allowed from July 1. Each program needs approval from the governor and the U.S. Department of Education.

Can employers get paid to train workers in the trades?

In Ohio, TechCred reimburses employers up to $1,000 per employee per credential, up to $30,000 per application period. Federal apprenticeship money mostly flows through states and intermediaries, such as the State Apprenticeship Expansion Formula grants.

Which grants can a nonprofit trades-training program apply for?

Home Depot Foundation Path to Pro Education Grants (up to $10,000 in gift cards, applications open now for awards starting February 2027) and Lowe's Foundation Gable Grants ($100,000–$1,000,000; 2026 windows closed). Private foundations that fund workforce training are listed on our foundations-funding-trades-training page.
