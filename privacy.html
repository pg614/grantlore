Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-30.

No tracking. Grantlore uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Theme setting. If you use the day/night toggle, your choice is stored in your browser's local storage on your device only.

Email list. If you join the waitlist or alerts, your email address is sent to Buttondown, our email provider, and used only to send Grantlore emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link. See Buttondown's privacy policy.

Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Foundation data is public record from IRS Form 990-PF filings.
