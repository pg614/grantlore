1. A nonprofit describes its mission: “We run a volunteer food pantry in Dayton, Ohio, serving about 300 families a month.”

2. The helper suggests matching foundations, with reasons. One match: Kettering Family Foundation (Dayton, OH), which says it accepts applications.

  • It paid 164 grants totaling $7,104,500, with a typical (median) grant of $20,000.
  • 9 of those grants list food, hunger, meals or pantries in the stated purpose.
  • 32 of 164 grants went to Ohio recipients.
