EMPLOYEE MATCHING PROGRAM INTERNAL COMPANY ELECTRONIC APPLICATION REQUIRED MATERIALS: COPY OF RECEIPT FOR DONATION THAT INCLUDES THE NAME, ADDRESS, WEBSITE ADDRESS AND TAX ID OF CHARITABLE ORGANIZATION AND AN ACH DEPOSIT FORM. 3. GRANT PROGRAM NO FORMAL APPLICATION, IF SELECTED FOR A GRANT THE GRANTEE MUST PROVIDE ORGANIZATIONAL NAME, ADDRESS, AND TAX ID ALONG WITH THE ACH DEPOSIT FORM. GRANTS ARE SELECTED INTERNALLY BASED ON STRATEGIC AND PHILANTHROPIC GOALS OF THE COMPANY EACH YEAR.
DeadlinesSCHOLARSHIPS - MAY 30TH; EMPLOYEE MATCHING - FIRST WEEK IN DECEMBER; GRANT PROGRAM - NONE.
RestrictionsAWARDS ARE LIMITED TO ELIGIBLE DEPENDENTS OF ENSIGN-BICKFORD INDUSTRIES & SUBISIDARIES (SCHOLARSHIPS), ELIGIBLE EMPLOYEES (MATCHING GIFTS), AND QUALIFIED TAX-EXEMPT ORGANIZATIONS (GRANTS), WITH RECIPIENTS SELECTED USING OBJECTIVE CRITERIA (E.G., NEED/MERIT FOR SCHOLARSHIPS AND CHARITABLE PURPOSE/QUALIFICATION FOR GRANTS). SCHOLARSHIPS AND MATCHING GIFTS ARE SUBJECT TO MAXIMUM ANNUAL AWARD AND PER-EMPLOYEE MATCH LIMITS AND ANY APPLICABLE FREQUENCY LIMITS. ALL AWARDS AND GRANTS MUST COMPLY WITH BOARD-APPROVED WRITTEN ELIGIBILITY AND FUNDING GUIDELINES.
