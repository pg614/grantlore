Form & materialsTHE ONLY ORGANIZATIONS WHICH ARE ELIGIBLE FOR GRANTS OR DISTRIBUTIONS ARE CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS, DONATIONS TO WHICH QUALIFY FOR A DEDUCTION UNDER THE INTERNAL REVENUE CODE. THIS IS SET OUT IN THE TRUST DOCUMENT CREATING THE JOHN R. SELMAN AND MAGGIE B. SELMAN CHARITABLE TRUST. IT IS NOT EXPECTED THERE WILL BE ANY RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN ANY ORGANIZATION RECEIVING A GRANT OR DISTRIBUTION AND THE JOHN R. SELMAN AND MAGGIE B. SELMAN CHARITABLE TRUST OR THE CO-TRUSTEES OF THE JOHN R. SELMAN AND MAGGIE B. SELMAN CHARITABLE TRUST.
DeadlinesSEE ABOVE
RestrictionsTHE GRANT PROPOSAL WILL OBLIGATE THE RECIPIENT ORGANIZATION TO USE THE GRANT OF FUNDS FOR WHICH THE GRANT WAS MADE. THE GRANT PROPOSAL WILL REQUIRE A REPORT TO BE MADE AS TO HOW THE FUNDS WERE USED AND PRODUCTION OF BOOKS AND/OR RECORDS BY THE GRANTEE SUBSTANTIATING THE USE OF THE GRANT OF FUNDS IN THE MANNER INTENDED UNDER THE GRANT PROPOSAL. THE GRANT PROPOSAL WILL ACKNOWLEDGE THE AUTHORITY OF THE JOHN R. SELMAN AND MAGGIE B. SELMAN CHARITABLE TRUST TO WITHHOLD OR RECOVER GRANT FUNDS WHEN IT APPEARS THE FUNDS WILL BE MISUSED OR HAVE BEEN MISUSED. FOLLOW-UPS WILL BE CONDUCTED BY THE JOHN R. SELMAN AND MAGGIE B. SELMAN CHARITABLE TRUST TO ENSURE THAT THE GRANTS ARE PROPERLY USED FOR THE PURPOSE INTENDED.
