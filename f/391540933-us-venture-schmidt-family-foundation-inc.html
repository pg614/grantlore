THE CEC GIVES STRONG CONSIDERATION TO REQUESTS WITHIN OUR GIVING GUIDELINES THAT SHOW DOCUMENTED SUPPORT FROM A U.S. VENTURE TEAM MEMBER OR SHAREHOLDER. U.S. VENTURE PREFERS NOT TO BE THE SOLE FUNDER. A LIST OF PENDING AND COMMITTED SPONSORS IS REQUIRED.
