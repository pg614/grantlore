How to apply

ContactMARIAN CONWAY
Address1400 OLD NORTHERN BLVD, ROSLYN, NY, 11576

Form & materialsTO BE CONSIDERED FOR A GRANT BY THE BOARD, AN APPLICANT MUST INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING ATTACHMENTS: 1-2 PAGE CONCISE COVER LETTER, WHICH WOULD INCLUDE: THE SPECIFIC REQUEST,APPROPRIATE BACK UP MATERIALS TO SUBSTANTIATE THE REQUEST E.G. A BUDGET, SCHEDULE, OR PROPOSED OUTCOME,GEOGRAPHIC REACH FOR THE GRANT E.G. TOWN, COUNTY, MATERIAL COVERING THE AGENCIES HISTORICAL BACKGROUND (1ST TIME APPLICANTS), AND COPY OF 501(C)3, COPY OF CURRENT 990 FORM, COPY OF CURRENT FINANCIAL AUDIT (PURSUANT TO NYS NONPROFIT REVITALIZATION ACT OF 2013 GUIDELINES), LIST OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS INCLUDING BUSINESS AFFILIATIONS, LIST OF CURRENT BUSINESS CONTRIBUTORS AND FOUNDATION GRANTS INCLUDING THE LEVEL OF SUPPORT.
DeadlinesALL PROPOSALS SHOULD BE SUBMITTED BY 10/15, AS MOST OF THE DISTRIBUTION IS DONE BY 11/30.
RestrictionsGRANTEES NEED TO SUBMIT APPLICATION FOR FUTURE GRANTS YEARLY, AS THE INFORMATION NEEDS TO BE UPDATED YEAR TO YEAR. THIS INCLUDES ORGANIZATIONS WITH MULTI-YEAR COMMITMENTS. THE FOUNDATION REQUIRES THAT AN AGENCY KEEP IT INFORMED ABOUT THE PROJECT IN WHICH IT WAS FUNDED AND ANY OTHER PERTINENT INFORMATION CONCERNING THE AGENCY ON AN ON-GOING BASIS. THE FOUNDATION MONITORS THE GRANTED MONIES BASED ON THE AGENCY UPDATES.
