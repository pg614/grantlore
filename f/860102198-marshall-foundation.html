HAVE A PROJECT THAT WILL TAKE PLACE IN PIMA COUNTY OR BENEFIT RESIDENTS OF PIMA COUNTY, AZ. HAVE SUBMITTED A FORMAL APPLICATION THROUGH AN ONLINE PROCESS MARSHALL FOUNDATION WILL NOT ACCEPT REQUESTS FOR SUPPORT FOR PROJECTS OUTSIDE OF PIMA COUNTY OR FOR SPECIFIC INDIVIDUALS. THE GRANT PERIOD IS ONE YEAR. ORGANIZATION MUST BE CURRENT WITH ALL REPORTING REQUIREMENTS.
