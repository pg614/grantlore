Form & materialsADDRESS REQUESTS FOR GRANTS TO THE FOLLOWING: MAILING DELIVERY: 4045 Five Forks Trickum Rd SW Ste B9 #305 Lilburn, GA 30047 PHYSICAL DELIVERY: 4045 Five Forks Trickum Rd SW Ste B9 #305 Lilburn, GA 30047 GRANT PROPOSALS SHOULD BE SUBMITTED IN WRITING (CANNOT BE SUBMITTED ELECTRONICALLY) AND INCLUDE A DEFINITIVE STATEMENT AS TO THE HISTORY OF THE PROPOSED GRANTEE ORGANIZATION, THE PURPOSE AND AMOUNT OF THE PROPOSED GRANT, CURRENT FINANCIAL STATEMENTS OF THE PROPOSED GRANTEE ORGANIZATION SHOWING INFORMATION REGARDING SUPPORT BY THE LOCAL PEOPLE AND MERCHANTS AS WELL AS ALL GOVERNMENTAL SUPPORT, WHETHER LOCAL, STATE OR FEDERAL AND A WRITTEN REPRESENTATION AS TO THE FEDERAL TAX EXEMPT STATUS OF THE PROPOSED GRANTEE ORGANIZATION (I.E. 501(C) (3)). THIS DOCUMENTATION MUST BE SUBMITTED WITH EACH GRANT PROPOSAL IN ORDER FOR THE GRANT TO BE PRESENTED TO THE FOUNDATION'S BOARD OF TRUSTEES. FAILURE TO DO SO WOULD DISQUALIFY THE PROPOSAL.
DeadlinesNO LATER THAN MARCH 1ST AND SEPTEMBER 1ST
RestrictionsTHE FOUNDATION GENERALLY RESTRICTS ITS GRANTS TO PROGRAMS AND INSTITUTIONS INVOLVED IN CHILD WELFARE, RELIGIOUS, CIVIC, HEALTH OR EDUCATIONAL ACTIVITIES. GRANTS ARE RESTRICTED TO THE STATE OF GEORGIA, EXCEPT FOR PROGRAMS CARRIED ON BY CHESTNUT HILL BENEVOLENT ASSOCIATION IN BOSTON, MASSACHUSETTS. ALL GRANTS ARE MADE TO TAX EXEMPT ORGANIZATIONS. IN THE CASE OF SCHOLARSHIP AWARDS, GRANTS ARE MADE TO THE RESPECTIVE EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTION, RATHER THAN TO INDIVIDUAL STUDENTS.
