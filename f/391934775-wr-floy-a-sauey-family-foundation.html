How to apply

ContactKAREN RIEBS
AddressC/O NORDIC GROUP 715 LYNN AVE, BARABOO, WI, 53913
Phone6083562130
Form & materialsAPPLICANTS SUBMIT INFORMATION FOR THE SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM THROUGH A MULTISTEP FORMAT. FIRST THEY SUBMIT A GENERAL APPLICATION WITH BASIC INFORMATION. THEY SEND IN OFFICIAL TRANSCRIPTS OF THEIR GRADES FROM HIGH SCHOOL AND/OR ANY COLLEGE CREDIT COURSES THEY MAY HAVE TAKEN. THEY ASK 2 PEOPLE TO WRITE RECOMMENDATIONS FOR THEM - 1 IS TO BE A TEACHER AND 1 IS TO BE A COMMUNITY MEMBER. LASTLY EACH STUDENT MUST KEYBOARD AN ANSWER TO AN ESSAY QUESTION WRITTEN BY THE SCHOLARSHIP SELECTION COMMITTEE. THIS ESSAY ANSWER IS KEYBOARDED AT THE STUDENT'S LOCAL NORDIC GROUP FACILITY.
DeadlinesTHERE ARE VARIOUS DEADLINES THROUGHOUT THE PROCESS.
RestrictionsTHE RESTRICTIONS ON THE SCHOLARSHIPS ARE THAT THE STUDENTS MUST BE CHILDREN OR LEGAL GUARDIANS OF ASSOCIATES OF THE NORDIC GROUP OF COMPANIES, INC. ALSO THE SCHOLARSHIPS ARE CHOSEN WITH AN EMPHASIS ON BUSINESS AND TECHNICAL DEGREES, BUT THE SELECTION COMMITTEE HAS CHOSEN STUDENTS IN ALL FIELDS. THE STUDENTS MUST BE UNDER 25 AND FIRST DEGREE AFTER HIGH SCHOOL APPLICANTS.
