BUILDING/CAPITAL CAMPAIGNS 7. MEDICAL RESEARCH THERE IS NO MINIMUM OR MAXIMUM SIZE FOR GRANTS. GENERALLY, MULTI-YEAR GRANTS WILL NOT BE CONSIDERED.
