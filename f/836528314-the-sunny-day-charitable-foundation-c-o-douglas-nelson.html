RestrictionsTHIS FOUNDATION WILL OPERATE MUCH LIKE A COMMUNITY FOUNDATION WITH THE MAJORITY OF DISTRIBUTIONS MADE TO LOCAL 501(C)3 ORGANIZATIONS. FIELDS OF INTEREST INCLUDE EARLY CHILDHOOD EDUCATION, SPECIAL PROGRAMS TO ENHANCE EDUCATION FOR K-12, SCHOLARSHIPS FOR HIGHER LEARNING. THE FOUNDATION WILL ALSO FOCUS ON PREVENTION OF ABUSE/CRUELTY TO WOMEN, CHILDREN, AND/OR ANIMALS. FURTHER CONSIDERATION ALSO GIVEN TO ORGANIZATIONS THAT SERVE THE DISABLED, AND/OR THOSE IN NEED OF MEDICAL CARE/TREATMENTS.
