RestrictionsTHE WIEBOLDT FOUNDATION LIMITS ITS GRANT MAKING TO THE CHICAGO METROPOLITAN AREA. THE FOUNDATION SUPPORTS MULTI-ISSUE COMMUNITY ORGANIZING GROUPS THAT WORK IN LOWINCOME NEIGHBORHOODS, THAT ARE ACCOUNTABLE TO NEIGHBORHOOD RESIDENTS, AND THROUGH WHICH PEOPLE ARE EMPOWERED TO HAVE A MAJOR VOICE IN SHAPING DECISIONS THAT AFFECT THEIR LIVES. THE WIEBOLDT FOUNDATION GENERALLY DOES NOT FUND INDIVIDUALS, STUDIES AND RESEARCH, CONFERENCES, CAPITAL DEVELOPMENT, OR DIRECT OR SOCIAL SERVICE PROGRAMS.
