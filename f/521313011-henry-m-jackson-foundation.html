How to apply

ContactMAURA SULLIVAN PROGRAM OFFICER
Address1200 FIFTH AVENUE SUITE 1450, SEATTLE, WA, 98101
Phone2066828565
Form & materialsSTRATEGIC INITIATIVES GRANTS ($10K+): APPLICANTS SUBMIT A LETTER OF INTENT (LOI), AND AFTER REVIEW MAY BE INVITED FOR A FULL PROPOSAL, WHICH INCLUDES: PROGRAM DESCRIPTION, GOALS/OUTCOMES, AND PLANS FOR EVALUATION; IMPLEMENTATION STRATEGY AND BUDGET; DESCRIPTION OF THE ORGANIZATION AND FINANCIAL STATEMENT; IRS 501(C)(3) DETERMINATION LETTER. OPPORTUNITY GRANTS (LESS THAN $10K): LOI WITH INFORMATION ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION; DETAILS ABOUT THE PROPOSED PROJECT; AND A PROJECT BUDGET. APPLICANTS CAN USE THE ONLINE APPLICATION SYSTEM OR SEND MATERIALS DIRECTLY TO THE FOUNDATION.
DeadlinesSTRATEGIC INITIATIVE GRANT APPLICATIONS QUARTERLY: JANUARY 1, APRIL 1, JULY 1, AND OCTOBER 1.
RestrictionsGRANTS MUST SUPPORT THE FOUNDATION'S GOALS OF LEADERSHIP, ENVIRONMENT, HUMAN RIGHTS AND/OR INTERNATIONAL AFFAIRS EDUCATION. SOME OF THE OPPORTUNITY GRANT FUNDS FOCUS ON GROUPS OPERATING IN SNOHOMISH COUNTY, WASHINGTON, IN ORDER TO FURTHER THE FOUNDATION'S PRIORITIES AND TO HONOR SENATOR JACKSON'S TIES TO EVERETT, WASHINGTON. APPLICANTS MUST BE BASED IN THE UNITED STATES AND REGISTERED AS A 501(C)(3) NON-PROFIT, TAX-EXEMPT ORGANIZATION. GRANTS ARE NOT MADE TO COVER OPERATING DEFICITS OR FOR CAPITAL EXPENDITURES.

Source: 990-PF Part XV line 2, tax year 2024.
