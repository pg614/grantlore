DeadlinesNOT AVAILABLE
RestrictionsMCMILLEN FAMILY FOUNDATION WILL CONSIDER GRANTS TO ASSIST ORGANIZATIONS SERVING THOSE INDIVIDUALS IN RECOVERY FROM DRUGS AND ALCOHOL. THE FOUNDATION WILL ALSO CONSIDER ORGANIZATIONS WHICH PROVIDE THE OPPORTUNITY TO RECEIVE THE EDUCATION NEEDED BY THOSE AFFECTED BY ALCOHOL, DRUGS, AND OTHER UNFORTUNATE CIRCUMSTANCES TO BUILD SOUND FINANCIAL, SPIRITUAL, AND PHYSICALLY HEALTHY LIVES FOR THEMSELVES AND THEIR FAMILIES. ORGANIZATION MUST BE CERTIFIED UNDER SECTION 501(C)(3) OF THE U.S. INTERNAL REVENUE CODE AND IS NOT A PRIVATE FOUNDATION, AS DEFINED IN SECTION 509 (A) OF THAT CODE.
