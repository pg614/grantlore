How to apply

ContactVicki Lederer
Address680B Parr Road, Wentzville, MO, 63385
Phone6363325636
Form & materialsMust complete an application and submit a 2-3 page essay detailing their interest and examples of their passion for their field of study and future career. Must also submit two personal reference letters.
DeadlinesThe submission deadline is March 15th each year. Final recipients will be chosen by May 1st.
RestrictionsUpwards of 20 scholarships ranging from $500-$4,000 are available to students living in the communities served by the Wentzville R-IV and Troy R-III school districts. Students must demonstrate a passion for their intended area of study at any accredited college, trade school, or university. Amounts are determined based on scores earned by demonstrating passion for their intended field of study through essay, portfolio, grades, and references. Upon final selection, the scholarships will be paid directly to the school following proof of enrollment, course schedule, and payment instructions. Recipients must submit annual reports as to their enrollment status and grades.
