How to apply

ContactSTEVE PETTY
Address14800 E OLD US 12, CHELSEA, MI, 48118
Phone7344334599
Form & materialsELECTRONIC SUBMISSION THROUGH THE "GRANTS" TAB ON THE WEBSITE. ALL INFORMATION RELATED TO GRANT APPLICATION IS POSTED ON THE WEBSITE. APPLICANTS WILL BE REQUIRED TO PROVIDE PROOF OF NON-PROFIT STATUS, RESPONSIBLE CONTACT, DEMONSTRATED NEED, PURPOSE, MISSION, BUDGET, METHOD OF MEASUREMENT, AND SUSTAINABILITY PLAN.
DeadlinesAPPROXIMATELY THE FIRST OF FEBRUARY, APRIL, JUNE, AUGUST, OCTOBER & DECEMBER.
RestrictionsGRANTS ARE LIMITED TO NON-PROFITS IN THE ORGANIZATION'S SERVICE AREA. INCLUDING CHELSEA, DEXTER, GRASS LAKE, MANCHESTER, AND STOCKBRIDGE. THE ORGANIZATION ONLY GRANTS TO NON-PROFITS RELATED TO THE ORGANIZATION'S VISION (EAT BETTER, MOVE MORE, AVOID UNHEALTHY SUBSTANCES, AND CONNECT WITH OTHERS IN HEALTHY WAYS).
