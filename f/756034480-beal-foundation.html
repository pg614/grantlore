How to apply

ContactELIZABETH DAVENPORT
Address104 S PECOS, MIDLAND, TX, 79701
Phone4326823753
Form & materialsTHE BEAL FOUNDATION GRANT APPLICATION IS ONLY AVAILABLE AT WWW.THEBEALFOUNDATION.ORG INFORMATION REQUIRED FOR A GRANT APPLICATION IS DETAILED ON THE WEBSITE, INCLUDING THE CRITERIA FOR QUALIFIED ORGANIZATIONS THAT CAN APPLY. APPLICANTS MUST SUBMIT A GRANT APPLICATION ONLINE BY FEBRUARY 15 FOR THE APRIL MEETING AND BY SEPTEMBER 15 FOR THE NOVEMBER MEETING.
DeadlinesFEBRUARY 15 FOR APRIL GRANTS AND SEPTEMBER 15 FOR NOVEMBER GRANTS.
RestrictionsGRANTS WILL BE CONSIDERED FOR AGENCIES INVOLVING EDUCATION, HEALTH, HUMANITIES, AND WELFARE IN THE MIDLAND, TEXAS GEOGRAPHIC AREA. QUALIFIED ORGANIZATIONS: ARE TAX-EXEMPT UNDER INTERNAL REVENUE CODE SECTION 501(C)(3), ARE FURTHER CLASSIFIED AS A PUBLIC CHARITY UNDER INTERNAL REVENUE CODE SECTION 509(A), AND PROVIDE A COPY OF THE IRS LETTER OF DETERMINATION RULING. APPLICANTS CLAIMING EXEMPTION AS A GOVERNMENT ENTITY MUST ALSO PROVIDE EVIDENCE OF THEIR TAX-EXEMPT STATUS. INCOMPLETE ONLINE APPLICATIONS WILL NOT BE CONSIDERED. ALL REQUIRED INFORMATION MUST BE SUBMITTED BY THE DEADLINE. ONLY ONLINE GRANT APPLICATIONS WILL BE ACCEPTED.
