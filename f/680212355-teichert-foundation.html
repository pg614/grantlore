How to apply

ContactALLEGRA PICKETT GRANTS MANAGER
AddressPO BOX 15002, SACRAMENTO, CA, 958511002
Phone9164843255
Form & materialsAll Applications must be submitted via GOapply. In addition to the applicant Organization's name, tax ID Number and a general description of proposed use of grant proceeds, applicants must submit the following: 1) Proposal narrative, 2) Board of directors/affiliations and key staff member bios, 3) Program Budget, 4) Operational budget for agency, and 5) Most recent financial statement (audited financial statement if the organization's gross revenue is $2 million or more, or a management report if the organization's gross revenue is less than $2 million).
DeadlinesAUGUST 2, 2024 AND FEBRUARY 3, 2025
RestrictionsTHE GEOGRAPHIC REACH OF THE FOUNDATION COINCIDES GENERALLY WITH THE AREAS IN WHICH TEICHERT CONSTRUCTION AND TEICHERT AGGREGATES DO BUSINESS. THE FOLLOWING COUNTIES ARE WITHIN THE SCOPE OF FOUNDATION GRANTING ACTIVITY: Alameda, Alpine, Amador, Butte, Calaveras, Colusa, Contra Costa, El Dorado, Fresno, Madera, Mariposa, Merced, Monterey, Nevada, Placer, Sacramento, San Benito, San Bernardino, San Diego, San Joaquin, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano, Stanislaus, Sutter, Tuolumne, Tulare, Washoe, Yolo, and Yuba. THE FOUNDATION WILL NOT CONSIDER GRANTS TO THE FOLLOWING TYPES OF ORGANIZATIONS OR FOR THE FOLLOWING PURPOSES:RELIGIOUS ORGANIZATIONS FOR EXPLICIT RELIGIOUS ACTIVITIES, AS DISTINGUISHED FROM SOCIAL OR EDUCATIONAL ACTIVITIES POLITICAL ORGANIZATIONS OR POLITICAL CAMPAIGNS FRATERNAL ORGANIZATIONS, SOCIETIES, OR ORDERS COURTESY ADVERTISING OR TICKETS FOR BENEFITS TELEPHONE SOLICITATIONS NATIONAL FUNDRAISING EFFORTS
