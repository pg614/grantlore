Form & materialsTHE ACTIVITIES OF THE FOUNDATION TO MAKE CHARITABLE GRANTS WILL FURTHER THE FOUNDATION'S CHARITABLE, SCIENTIFIC, RELIGIOUS, LITERARY AND EDUCATIONAL PURPOSES. THE FOUNDATION MAY SELECT RECIPIENTS BASED ON GRANT REQUESTS FROM PUBLIC CHARITIES AND GOVERNMENTAL AGENCIES. ALL FORMAL AND INFORMAL GRANT PROPOSALS SHOULD BE ADDRESSED TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS INCLUDING THE PURPOSE OF THE GRANT REQUEST AND CONTACT INFORMATION FOR ACCEPTANCE OR DENIAL PURPOSES.
