Form & materialsTHE TED AND GRACE BACHHUBER FOUNDATION DOES NOT HAVE A FORM TO BE FILLED OUT BY A GRANT APPLICANT. A NARRATIVE PROPOSAL DESCRIBING: THE PLAN OR DESIGN FOR THE PROGRAM INCLUDING ACTIVITIES TO BE CARRIED OUT AND OBJECTIVES TO BE ACHIEVED; AND THE PLAN FOR CONTINUING THE PROJECT AFTER THE TERMINATION OF FOUNDATION SUPPORT 2. ORGANIZATIONS MUST BE CLASSIFIED 509A1 OR 509A2 5. A LIST OF THE MEMBERS OF THE GOVERNING BOARD.
DeadlinesNONE
RestrictionsTHE TED AND GRACE BACHHUBER FOUNDATION SEEKS TO SUPPORT PROGRAMS AND ORGANIZATIONS THAT IMPROVE THE QUALITY OF LIFE IN THE MAYVILLE AREA. GRANTS MADE BY THE FOUNDATION REFLECT THE BOARDS INTENTION TO ASSIST THE MAYVILLE AREA THROUGH THE FINANCIAL PROMOTION OF EDUCATIONAL CHARITABLE AND OTHER WORTHWHILE AND VIABLE PROJECTS. THESE PROJECTS MUST OFFER OPPORTUNITIES FOR ENRICHED LIFE TO A SUBSTANTIAL NUMBER OF PEOPLE OR PROVIDE MEMBERS OF THE COMMUNITY WITH SKILLS KNOWLEDGE AND ASSISTANCE TO ACCOMPLISH POSITIVE SOCIAL GOALS. GRANTS WILL BE MADE ONLY TO ORGANIZATIONS OR TO CAUSES WHOSE OBJECTIVES ARE CLEARLY DEFINED. GRANTS WILL NOT BE MADE TO POLITICAL ORGANIZATIONS OR FOR POLITICAL PURPOSES. GRANTS WILL NOT BE MADE TO INDIVIDUALS.
