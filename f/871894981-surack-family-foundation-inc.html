Form & materialsTHE APPLICATION FORM IS LOCATED ON THE FOUNDATION'S WEBSITE. THE FORM ASKS GENERAL QUESTIONS ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION, THE PURPOSE OF THE GRANT REQUEST AND HOW IT WILL BE USED. SINCE THE FOUNDATION ONLY PROVIDES GRANTS TO NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS WITH 501(C)(3) STATUS, AN IRS DETERMINATION LETTER MUST BE PROVIDED WITH THE APPLICATION.
DeadlinesTHERE ARE NO SUBMISSION DEADLINES.
RestrictionsTHE FOUNDATION MAKES GRANTS ONLY TO 501(C)(3) ORGANIZATIONS WHOSE GEOGRAPHIC FOCUS WILL BE IN NORTHEAST INDIANA AND WHOSE GRANT REQUEST IS DIRECTLY RELATED TO MENTAL HEALTH, CHILD & FAMILY WELFARE, MUSIC & THE ARTS OR ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT.
