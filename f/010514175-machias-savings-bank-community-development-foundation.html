How to apply

ContactMelissa Moffett Denbow Community Ex
AddressPO Box 318, Machias, ME, 04654
Phone2072559266
Emailmdenbow@machiassavings.com
Form & materialsPotential applicants may learn about and apply for funding through the Machias Savings Bank website, at: https://www.machiassavings.bank/donations-and-sponsorships-guidelines/ Applicants will be asked to describe their organization and why they are deserving of funding. The Foundation will prioritize projects that help improve the lives of our community members. Priorities are given to the following: financial health, basic needs, low to moderate income programming, workforce development, entrepreneurship, broadband connectivity, childcare, and youth programming. The applicant must demonstrate the ability to carry out the objectives cited in the grant application. Applicants will need to attach all supporting documentation and include a copy of their 501(c)(3) determination letter from the IRS.
DeadlinesThe Foundation grants are awarded twice each year.
RestrictionsThe Foundation only makes grants to qualified 501(c)(3) organizations. A nonprofit may apply for a donation from the Machias Savings Bank and still be eligible for a grant from the Foundation. Awards are generally limited to organizations operating in the service areas of Machias Savings Bank. The Foundation does not award donations to religious or political organizations, unless the programming is for food insecurity or to advance financial health. The Foundation also does not award donations to individuals.
