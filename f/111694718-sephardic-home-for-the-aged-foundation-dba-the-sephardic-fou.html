How to apply

Form & materialsAPPLICANT ORGANIZATIONS CREATE AN ACCOUNT WITH THE FOUNDATION'S GRANT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM AND COMPLETE AND SUBMIT A LETTER OF INQUIRY (LOI) FORM VIA THE GRANT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM. LOI'S ARE THEN REVIEWED BY STAFF AND PRESENTED FOR REVIEW TO THE GRANTS COMMITTEE OF THE BOARD. THE GRANTS COMMITTEE REVIEWS THE LOIS AND SELECTED APPLICANT ORGANIZATIONS ARE INVITED TO SUBMIT A FULL APPLICATION. FULL APPLICATIONS ARE REVIEWED BY STAFF AND PRESENTED TO GRANTS COMMITTEE, WHO THEN MAKE RECOMMENDATIONS TO THE FULL BOARD. THE FULL BOARD THEN REVIEWS THE APPLICATIONS AND VOTES ON THE GRANTS COMMITTEE'S RECOMMENDATIONS.
