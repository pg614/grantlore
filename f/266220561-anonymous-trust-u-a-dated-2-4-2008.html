Form & materialsAPPLICANTS COMMITTING TO A SIX WEEK FULL TIME SUMMER PROJECT MUST SUBMIT AN ONLINE APPLICATION, RESUME, DESCRIPTION OF THE PROJECT, A BUDGET, TIMELINE, AND A COPY OF A VALID NORTH CAROLINA TEACHING LICENSE ACCOMPANIED BY A RECOMMENDATION LETTER.
DeadlinesPUBLICIZED ON THE SIMPLE GIFTS WEBSITE AND FLYERS TO SAMPSON COUNTY AND CLINTON CITY SCHOOLS.
RestrictionsTHE ONE TIME GRANTS ARE FOR $10,000 EACH AND WILL NOT BE RENEWED. THE GRANTS ARE BASED SOLELY ON MERIT AND THE PROPOSALS WILL BE JUDGED ON SUBSTANCE, CLARITY, FEASIBILITY AND RESEARCH. THE SELECTION COMMITTEE WILL REVIEW THE EXPECTED DIRECT AND INDIRECT BENEFITS OF THE GRANT PROPOSAL TO THE TEACHER, HIS OR HER STUDENTS AND THE SCHOOL SYSTEM. THE GRANTS ARE AVAILABLE TO TEACHERS, GUIDANCE COUNSELORS AND LIBRARY MEDIA SPECIALISTS THAT HAVE BEEN EMPLOYED BY SAMPSON COUNTY OR CLINTON CITY SCHOOLS FOR AT LEAST TWO YEARS. THE APPLICANT MUST BE NORTH CAROLINA CERTIFIED IN GRADES K-12 AND BE EMPLOYED FULL TIME. THE GRANTS WILL BE TAXABLE TO THE RECIPIENT AND WILL STATE THAT THE RECIPIENT WILL REPAY THE TRUST IF THE PROJECT CANNOT BE COMPLETED.

Form & materialsAPPLICANTS SHOULD SUBMIT THE ONLINE APPLICATION (AVAILABLE AT WEBSTITE WWW.SIMPLEGIFTSFUND.ORG), RESUME, A DESCRIPTION OF THE PROPOSED PROJECT THAT MUST INCLUDE A TITLE AND SUMMARY OF 50 WORDS OR LESS, A NARRATIVE OF 500 WORDS OR LESS THAT EXPLAINS THE NUMBER OF STUDENTS SERVED, WHAT THE STUDENTS WILL LEARN, HOW AND WHERE THE STUDENTS WILL LEARN, WHY THE PROJECT IS NEEDED, WHEN THE STUDENTS WILL LEARN (TIMELINE), HOW THE PROJECT'S SUCCESS WILL BE EVALUATED AND HOW THE PROJECT IMPROVES THE STUDENT'S KNOWLEDGE AND SKILLS. IN ADDITION, A LINE ITEM BUDGET FOR THE PROPOSED GRANT MUST BE SUBMITTED. IF EDUCATOR STIPENDS ARE INCLUDED IN THE GRANT, TWO SEPARATE AMOUNTS SHOULD BE INDICATED IN THE BUDGET AND TWO CHECKS WILL BE ISSUED (ONE TO GRANTEE AND ONE TO THE FISCAL AGENT). THE EDUCATOR WILL RECEIVE AN IRS INCOME REPORTING 1099MISC FOR THE STIPEND. ANY ADDITIONAL SUPPORT PROVIDED BY OTHER SOURCES MUST BE IDENTIFIED.
DeadlinesPUBLICIZED ON THE SIMPLE GIFTS WEBSITE AND FLYERS TO SAMPSON COUNTY AND CLINTON CITY SCHOOLS
RestrictionsGRANTS FOR SCHOOLS UP TO $3,000 & INDIVIDUAL STIPENDS UP TO $1,000 WILL BE AWARDED 3 TIMES ANNUALLY FOR THE ADVANCEMENT OF EDUCATORS/STUDENTS, TO ENABLE TEACHERS TO PROVIDE ILLUMINATING PROGRAMS/OPPORTUNITIES FOR STUDENTS IN THE CLASSROOM, AFTER SCHOOL, SUMMER OR WEEKENDS. APPLICANTS MUST BE A 10 MONTH TEACHER, GUIDANCE COUNSELOR OR MEDIA COORDINATOR IN SAMPSON COUNTY OR CLINTON CITY PUBLIC K-12; HAVE 2 YEARS TEACHING EXPERIENCE; A FULL TIME EDUCATOR SPENDING AT LEAST 50% OF THE TIME IN THE CLASSROOM/CLASSROOM-LIKE SETTING; RETURNING TO TEACH IN SAMPSON COUNTY IN THE NEXT SCHOOL YEAR & HAVE THE PROPOSAL REVIEWED/APPROVED BY THE PRINCIPAL. FUNDS MAY BE USED FOR RESOURCE MATERIALS, SUPPLIES, EQUIPMENT, TRANSPORTATION, TECHNOLOGY OR STIPENDS, THE MAJORITY MUST BE SPENT ON MATERIALS/EDUCATIONAL EXPERIENCES FOR STUDENTS WITH NO LOBBYING/RELIGIOUS PURPOSES.
