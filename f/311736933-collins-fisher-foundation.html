Form & materialsTHERE IS NO SPECIAL FORM FOR REQUESTS OF CONTRIBUTIONS OR GIFTS FOR PUBLIC CHARITABLE PURPOSES, WHICH INCLUDES CHARITABLE, SCIENTIFIC, LITERARY, RELIGIOUS, AND EDUCATIONAL PUBLIC PURPOSES. A SIMPLE LETTER SETTING FORTH PUBLIC PURPOSE IS SUFFICIENT.
DeadlinesREQUESTS ARE RECEIVED ANYTIME. REQUEST FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION IS MADE WITHIN A REASONABLE TIME.
RestrictionsTHERE IS NO RESTRICTION ON GEOGRAPHICAL AREAS. CONTRIBUTIONS, GIFTS, AND AWARDS ARE MADE EXCLUSIVELY IN AID OF PUBLIC CHARITABLE PURPOSES. THIS INCLUDSE SUCH CHARITABLE, SCIENTIFIC, LITERARY, RELIGIOUS, AND EDUCATIONAL OTHER PURPOSES. NO SCHOLARSHIPS, LOANS, OR GRANTS FOR FURTHER STUDIES AND RESEARCH ARE MADE TO INDIVIDUALS. THE FOUNDATION MAKES CONTRIBUTIONS TO SOME PRE-SELECTED ORGANIZATIONS. THERE ARE DOLLAR LIMITATIONS DEPENDING ON THE AVAILABILITY OF FUNDS AND THE CHOICE OF THE TRUSTEES OF THE PROJECT THAT MOST BENEFITS THE PUBLIC CHARITABLE PURPOSE.
