RestrictionsA POTENTIAL GRANTEE ORGANIZATION MUST BE A NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION LOCATED WITHIN 10 MILES OF THE BOROUGH OF POTTSTOWN. THE GRANT MUST FALL WITHIN ONE OF THE ORGANIZATION'S THREE CORE PRIORITIES OF: (1) FUNDING PROGRAMS AND SOLUTIONS FOR LONG-TERM IMPROVEMENT IN HEALTHY BEHAVIORS; (2) FUNDING PROGRAMS FOR PHYSICAL HEALTH AND EMOTIONAL WELL-BEING; (3) FUNDING LEARNING OPPORTUNITIES AND STRATEGIC PLANNING TO STRENGTHEN NON-PROFITS.
