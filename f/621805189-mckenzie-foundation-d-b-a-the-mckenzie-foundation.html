How to apply

This foundation checked Part XV line 2 of its 990-PF: it only makes contributions to preselected charitable organizations and does not accept unsolicited requests.

ContactW THORPE MCKENZIE
Address832 GEORGIA AVENUE, CHATTANOOGA, TN, 37402
Phone4232663544
Form & materialsTHE APPLICATION SHOULD BE SUBMITTED ON THE FORM DESIGNED BY MCKENZIE FOUNDATION. THE APPLICATION SHOULD INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING INFORMATION: PARENT/GUARDIAN NAMES AND INCOME LEVEL; EXTRACURRICULAR ACTIVITIES; HONORS OR AWARDS RECEIVED; WORK EXPERIENCE; SCHOOL PLANNING TO ATTEND; WHETHER ANY OTHER SCHOLARSHIPS HAVE BEEN APPLIED FOR; AND WHY THEY ARE APPLYING FOR THIS SCHOLARSHIP. APPLICANTS SHOULD INCLUDE AN OFFICIAL HIGH SCHOOL TRANSCRIPT. RENEWAL APPLICANTS SHOULD INCLUDE: A TRANSCRIPT FROM THE SCHOOL THEY ARE ATTENDING FOR THE IMMEDIATELY PRECEEDING PERIOD.
RestrictionsTHE GRANT MUST BE USED FOR EDUCATION AT A SECONDARY SCHOOL, COLLEGE, OR UNIVERSITY. THE RECIPIENT MUST REMAIN IN GOOD STANDING WITH THE SCHOOL.
