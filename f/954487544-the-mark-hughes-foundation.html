Form & materialsTHE MARK HUGHES FOUNDATION IS INTERESTED IN PROVIDING FOR CHILDREN IN NEED IN THE SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA AREA, PRIMARILY LOS ANGELES COUNTY. ORGANIZATIONS SEEKING GRANTS SHOULD SEND A LETTER OF INQUIRY INCLUDING THE HISTORY AND MISSION OF THE ORGANIZATION; THE PROJECT FOR WHICH FUNDING IS SOUGHT; AND AN AMOUNT WHICH IS BEING SOUGHT FROM THE FOUNDATION. THE LETTER SHOULD BE ON YOUR LETTERHEAD AND SIGNED; ADDRESSED TO ANN VAN DORMOLEN, ADMINISTRATOR, THE MARK HUGHES FOUNDATION, PO BOX 9399, MARINA DEL REY, CA 90295 AND MAILED, OR ATTACHED TO AN EMAIL TO ANN@PAISERVICES.COM.
