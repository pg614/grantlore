How to apply

ContactRobert Glenn Rapp Foundation
Address5400 N Grand Blvd Ste 545, Oklahoma City, OK, 73112
Phone4055258331
Form & materialsThe foundation has a website and in order to apply for a grant the Charitable Organization must submit a "Letter of Inquiry". The Letter of Inquiry is available on the Rapp Foundation website (www.rappok.org) and must be submitted from April 1 through May 31st of each year. Late Inquiries will not be allowed.
DeadlinesLetter of Inquiry per website from April 1 through May 31.
RestrictionsEducational, Scientific, Religious, or Literacy purpose is required. A 501(c)(3)letter is required.
