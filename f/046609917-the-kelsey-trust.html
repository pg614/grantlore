Form & materialsTHE TRUST FUNDS A LIMITED NUMBER OF UNSOLICITED PROPOSALS THROUGH A LETTER OF INTEREST APPLICATION WHICH PROVIDES: 1. A SUMMARY OF THE PROPOSAL AND ITS STRATEGIC FIT WITH KELSEY TRUST'S MISSION AND PROGRAM PRIORITIES 2. A STATEMENT OF THE PROPOSAL'S TARGET POPULATION AND GEOGRAPHIC COMMUNITIES SERVED 3. A STATEMENT OF THE PROJECT'S EXPECTED OUTCOMES AND HOW THE ORGANIZATION - AND THE TRUST - WILL KNOW IF THE PROGRAM IS SUCCESSFUL 4. THE AMOUNT REQUESTED FROM THE KELSEY TRUST AND THE PERCENTAGE OF THE PROPOSED PROJECT/PROGRAM'S BUDGET SUCH AN AMOUNT WOULD REPRESENT 5. THE PERIOD OVER WHICH THE REQUESTED GRANT WOULD BE EXPENDED 6. A LIST OF OTHER SUPPORT, BOTH PUBLIC AND PRIVATE, RECEIVED FOR THE PROPOSED PROJECT/PROGRAM THE LETTER OF REQUEST SHOULD INCLUDE AN ORGANIZATION STATEMENT ON THE ORGANIZATION'S LETTERHEAD THAT SETS FORTH THE FOLLOWING: 1. THE LEGAL NAME OF THE ORGANIZATION, ADDRESS, AND NAME OF THE EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR 2. INDICATION OF WHETHER THE ORGANIZATION IS AN IRS 501(C)(3)
DeadlinesSPRING GRANT - TYPICALLY FOCUSED ON EDUCATION AND THE ENVIRONMENT. THE LETTER OF INTEREST DEADLINE I
RestrictionsTHE TRUST'S GIVING IS LIMITED TO ORGANIZATIONS THAT SERVE THE PEOPLE AND PROTECT THE NATURAL ENVIRONMENT OF THE LAKE CHAMPLAIN BASIN.
