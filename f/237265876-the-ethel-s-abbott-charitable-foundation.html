How to apply

This foundation checked Part XV line 2 of its 990-PF: it only makes contributions to preselected charitable organizations and does not accept unsolicited requests.

ContactDOROTHY PFLUG - PRESIDENT
AddressPO BOX 27008, RALSTON, NE, 68127
Phone4023394899
EmailETHELSABBOTTFOUNDATION@GMAIL.COM
Form & materialsORGANIZATIONS THAT WANT TO REQUEST A GRANT FROM THE ETHEL S. ABBOTT CHARITABLE FOUNDATION SHOULD FILL IN THE GRANT INTEREST FORM ON THE FOUNDATION'S WEBSITE (WWW.ABBOTTFOUNDATION.ORG). IF THE ORGANIZATION'S GRANT INTEREST FORM IS APPROVED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES, THE ORGANIZATION WILL BE E-MAILED A GRANT APPLICATION FORM.
DeadlinesAPPLICATIONS MUST BE RETURNED TO THE CHARITABLE FOUNDATION WITHIN 30 DAYS AFTER RECEIVING THE FORM.
RestrictionsGRANT AWARDS ARE LIMITED TO THE AMOUNT REQUESTED, OR THE AMOUNT WHICH THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES DEEMS NECESSARY TO MEET THE NEED EXPRESSED IN THE GRANT APPLICATION SUBMITTED TO THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES FOR ITS CONSIDERATION.
