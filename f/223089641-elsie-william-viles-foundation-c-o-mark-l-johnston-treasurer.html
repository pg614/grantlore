How to apply

ContactPatricia A West
AddressElsie and William Viles Foundation, PO Box 319, Augusta, ME, 043320319
Phone2076221124
Emailinfo@elsieandwilliamvilesfoundation.org
Form & materialsThe application form, submission materials, and other application information can be found online at: https://www.elsieandwilliamvilesfoundation.org/Application/ To request funding, applicants should be prepared to submit a cover letter, proof of 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status, and a proposal for the project or purpose which requires funding. Applicants may also be requested to provide budgets and reports regarding the use of any received funding.
DeadlinesApplications open April and are due by May. Recipients are notified in July.
RestrictionsThe Foundation only provides funding for charitable, scientific, literary or educational purposes as are permitted to organizations exempt from federal income tax and described in section 501(c)(3) of the code, including without limitation, projects and organizations that: Preserve open spaces and conservation of forest lands; care for and protect animals; support children and education; and promote and preserve Maine History and culture.
