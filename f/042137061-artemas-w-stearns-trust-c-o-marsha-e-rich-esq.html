DeadlinesJANUARY 31 OF THE YEAR FOR WHICH THE GRANTS ARE REQUESTED.
RestrictionsGRANT RECIPIENTS WILL BE REQUIRED TO SIGN A RECEIPT AND ASSENT IN THE FORM APPROVED BY THE TRUST. IN ADDITION, THE ORGANIZATION MUST CERTIFY TO THE TRUST THAT THE FUNDS HAVE BEEN EXPENDED FOR THE PURPOSES FOR WHICH THE GRANT WAS MADE AND DESCRIBE THE DISPOSITION OF THE FUNDS NO LATER THAN DECEMBER 31 OF THE YEAR IN WHICH THE GRANT WAS MADE. IF THE FUNDS HAVE NOT BEEN FULLY SPENT BY DECEMBER 31, THE ORGANIZATION MUST SEND A LETTER BY THAT DATE STATING HOW MUCH OF THE GRANT HAS BEEN SPENT AND THAT THE AMOUNT SPENT FOR THE PURPOSES FOR WHICH THE GRANT WAS MADE. FUTURE GRANT REQUESTS FROM THE ORGANIZATION WHICH DO NOT COMPLY WITH THIS REQUIREMENT WILL NOT BE CONSIDERED BY THE TRUSTEES.
