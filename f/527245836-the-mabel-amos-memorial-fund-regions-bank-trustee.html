Form & materialsA LINK IS PROVIDED TO HIGH SCHOOL COUNSELORS IN FEBRUARY FOR THEM TO PROVIDE TO STUDENTS INTERESTED IN APPLYING FOR A SCHOLARSHIP. THE STUDENT THEN CREATES AN ONLINE ACCOUNT AND COMPLETES THE APPLICATION FORM. THE STUDENT'S HIGH SCHOOL TRANSCRIPT MUST BE PROVIDED. THE COURT IN A PENDING LITIGATION MATTER INVOLVING MABEL AMOS MEMORIAL FUND ENTERED AN ORDER APPOINTING A SPECIAL FIDUCIARY AND DIRECTING THAT SCHOLARSHIP APPLICATIONS WOULD BE ACCEPTED, REVIEWED, AND DETERMINED BY THE SPECIAL FIDUCIARY UNTIL SUCH TIME AS HE IS RELIEVED OF HIS DUTIES. ACCORDINGLY, 2025 SCHOLARSHIP AWARDS WERE DETERMINED BY THE SPECIAL FIDUCIARY PURSUANT TO SUCH ORDER.
DeadlinesTYPICALLY IN EARLY APRIL. DATE SUBJECT TO CHANGE EACH YEAR.
RestrictionsDISTRIBUTIONS ARE LIMITED TO THE PROVIDING OF SCHOLARSHIPS TO DESERVING YOUNG MEN AND WOMEN OF THE STATE OF ALABAMA TO ASSIST THEM IN ATTENDING ANY EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTION.
