DATE ESTABLISHED AND ANTICIPATED DURATION)4) ORGANIZATION'S ANNUAL BUDGET AND DETAILED PROJECT BUDGET WITH AMOUNT REQUESTED5) COPY OF THE ORGANIZATION'S IRS EXEMPTION LETTERLETTERS OF INQUIRY ARE REVIEWED WITHIN 60 DAYS OF RECEIPT. IF THE PROJECT FALLS WITHIN THE INTERESTS OF THE FOUNDATION, THE APPLICANT WILL BE ADVISED TO SUBMIT A COMPLETE PROPOSAL, INCLUDING A LISTING OF WHAT ADDITIONAL INFORMATION IS REQUIRED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS. COMPLETED APPLICATIONS WILL BE DISCUSSED AT THE NEXT SCHEDULED BOARD OF DIRECTORS' MEETING, PROVIDED ALL THE REQUESTED INFORMATION IS RECEIVED AT LEAST 30 DAYS PRIOR TO THE MEETING. APPLICANTS WILL BE NOTIFIED OF THE DISPOSITION OF THE REQUESTS WITHIN 2 WEEKS AFTER THE BOARD MEETING.
DeadlinesAPPLICATIONS AND LETTER OF INQUIRY ACCEPTED AT ANY TIME.
RestrictionsPREFERENCE IS GIVEN TO KANSAS RESIDENTS AND NOT-FOR-PROFIT ORGANIZATIONS WHICH HAVE IRS EXEMPTION LETTER.
