Form & materialsALL FELLOWSHIP APPLICATIONS MUST BE MADE VIA A LINK PROVIDED AT WWW.FRANCISFELLOWSHIPS.ORG AND SHOULD INCLUDE: (A) GENERAL DESCRIPTION OF THE CURRENT RESEARCH ACTIVITIES IN THE PULMONARY UNIT; (B) SUMMARY OF PAST TRAINING RECORD OF THE UNIT; (C) BIOGRAPHICAL SKETCH OF THE CANDIDATE FELLOW; (D) OUTLINE OF THE RESEARCH PROJECT TO BE UNDERTAKEN; (E) ENDORSEMENT OF THE APPLICATION BY THE APPROPRIATE DEPARTMENT HEAD AND BY AN AUTHORIZED OFFICIAL OF THE INSTITUTION AGREEING TO ADMINISTER THE GRANT.
DeadlinesINQUIRE WITH CONTACT
RestrictionsFELLOWSHIPS ARE AWARDED IN THE FIELD OF PULMONARY MEDICINE. THE DIRECTOR OF ANY PULMONARY TRAINING PROGRAM MAY APPLY ON BEHALF OF THE FELLOW. AWARDS ARE LIMITED TO INSTITUTIONS LOCATED IN THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA. A DEPARTMENT MAY SUBMIT NO MORE THAN TWO (2) APPLICATIONS ANNUALLY. DETAILED ELIGIBILITY AND EVALUATION CRITERIA CAN BE FOUND ON THE FOUNDATION'S WEBSITE AT: WWW.FRANCISFELLOWSHIPS.ORG/ELIGIBILITY-EVALUATION-CRITERIA

RestrictionsTO BE ELIGIBLE FOR A SMALL ARTS GRANT, AN ORGANIZATION SHOULD BE: (1) LOCATED WITHIN THE GREATER KANSAS CITY AREA (60-MILE RADIUS), (2) A TAX-EXEMPT 501(C)(3) PUBLIC CHARITY WITH A FEDERAL EIN, OR PARTNER WITH ANOTHER ORGANIZATION THAT CAN SERVE AS A FISCAL SPONSOR, AND (3) AN ARTS ORGANIZATION WITH ANNUAL REVENUES OF LESS THAN $300,000 LAST FISCAL YEAR.
