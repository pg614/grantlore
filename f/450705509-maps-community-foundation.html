How to apply

Form & materialsAPPLICANTS MAY SUBMIT A WRITTEN APPLICATION AND PHOTO ON THE FOUNDATION'S WEBSITE (MAPSCU.COM/COMMUNITY-INVOLVEMENT/TEACHER-GRANTS). DESCRIBE HOW THE AWARD WILL BE USED, THE NUMBER OF STUDENTS IMPACTED, THE TOTAL COST OF THE PROJECT, AND MOST IMPORTANTLY, HOW WILL THIS GRANT MAKE A DIFFERENCE TO THE APPLICANT'S STUDENTS.
DeadlinesTHE APPLICATION PERIOD IS OCTOBER 1ST - OCTOBER 31ST.
RestrictionsAPPLICANTS MUST BE K-12 PUBLIC AND PRIVATE SCHOOL TEACHERS IN MARION AND POLK COUNTIES.
ContactMAPS COMMUNITY FOUNDATION
Address451 DIVISION ST, SALEM, OR, 97303
Phone5035880181
EmailKHANSON@MAPSCU.COM
Form & materialsSPONSORSHIP REQUEST FORM MUST BE COMPLETED. THE FORM MAY BE FOUND ON THE FOUNDATION'S WEBSITE (MAPSCU.COM/COMMUNITY-INVOLVEMENT/MAPS-COMMUNITY-FOUNDATION/SPONSORSHIP-REQU ESTS).
DeadlinesREQUESTS MUST BE SUBMITTED AT LEAST 30 DAYS PRIOR TO THE FUNDING BEING NEEDED.
RestrictionsTHE FOUNDATION WILL CONSIDER SPONSORING COMMUNITY EVENTS THAT CONTRIBUTE TO COMMUNITY VIBRANCY AND ADD VALUE TO THE COMMUNITY, OR THAT CREATE POSITIVE COMMUNITY IMPACT FOR INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES LIVING IN POLK AND MARION COUNTIES. SPONSORSHIPS ARE ONLY FOR LOCAL NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATIONS AND MUST RELATE TO ONE OF THE THREE MAPS COMMUNITY FOUNDATION IMPACT AREAS: EDUCATION, ECONOMIC EMPOWERMENT, AND/OR VIBRANT COMMUNITIES. STATEWIDE OR NATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS MUST HAVE A MARION AND/OR POLK COUNTY PRESENCE TO BE CONSIDERED. MAPS COMMUNITY FOUNDATION WILL NOT FUND INDIVIDUALS, RELIGIOUS ORGANIZATIONS, OR POLITICAL ACTIVITIES.
