RestrictionsTHE FOUNDATION GENERALLY ADHERES TO THE FOLLOWING GUIDELINES. 1. THE GEOGRAPHIC AREAS FOR GRANT CONSIDERATION ARE NORMALLY LIMITED TO COMMUNITIES WITHIN THE UNITED STATES WHERE LA-Z-BOY INCORPORATED MANUFACTURING FACILITIES ARE LOCATED AND WITHIN A 30 MILE RADIUS OF CORPORATE HEADQUARTERS. 2. THE FOUNDATION DOES NOT MAKE DIRECT GRANTS TO INDIVIDUALS. 3. THE FOUNDATION DOES NOT MAKE GRANTS FOR TRAVEL AND CONFERENCES. 4. THE FOUNDATION DOES NOT MAKE LOANS. 5. THE FOUNDATION DOES NOT PROVIDE STARTUP FUNDS OF SEED MONEY.
