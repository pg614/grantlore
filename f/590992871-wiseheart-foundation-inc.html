How to apply

ContactWISEHEART FOUNDATION
Address2840 SW 3RD AVENUE, MIAMI, FL, 33129
Phone3052851222
Form & materialsTHE WISEHEART FOUNDATION DOES NOT USE GRANT APPLICATION FORMS. A PROPOSAL SHOULD BE SUBMITTED IN WRITING ON THE LETTERHEAD OF THE ORGANIZATION REQUESTING THE GRANT AND SIGNED BY ITS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER ON BEHALF OF ITS GOVERNING BODY. THE FOLLOWING INFORMATION SHOULD BE INCLUDED: THE NEED WHICH WOULD BE MET BY THE GRANT REQUESTED, THE MANNER IN WHICH THE NEED WOULD BE MET BY THE PROJECT OR PROGRAM PROPOSED, THE REASONS WHICH WOULD INDICATE THAT THE ORGANIZATION REQUESTING THE GRANT IS THE LOGICAL ONE TO CARRY OUT THE PROJECT, THE QUALIFICATIONS OF THE STAFF LEADERSHIP OF THE ORGANIZATION, A PROPOSED BUDGET FOR THE PROJECT, PROPOSED METHODS FOR EVALUATING THE PROJECT, AN INDICATION AS TO HOW THE PROGRAM WILL BE SUPPORTED AFTER THE REQUESTED GRANT EXPIRES, AND BACKGROUND INFORMATION OF THE ORGANIZATION AND ITS DIRECTORS AND OFFICERS.
DeadlinesBY NOVEMBER 30 FOR CONSIDERATION IN THE CURRENT YEAR.
RestrictionsTHE WISEHEART FOUNDATION IS WILLING TO PROVIDE FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO OTHER ORGANIZATIONS SHARING THE SAME OR SIMILAR CHARITABLE OBJECTIVES, PROVIDED THAT SUCH ORGANIZATIONS ARE ESTABLISHED FOR NON-PROFIT PURPOSES AND HAVE BEEN FORMALLY RECOGNIZED BY THE INTERNAL REVENUE SERVICE AS ORGANIZATIONS DESCRIBED IN SECTION 501(C)(3) AND FURTHER PROVIDED THAT SUCH ORGANIZATIONS ARE NOT PRIVATE FOUNDATIONS WITHIN THE MEANING OF "SECTION 509(A) (2)." THE WISEHEART FOUNDATION DOES NOT MAKE GRANTS DIRECTLY TO INDIVIDUALS. STRONG PREFERENCE IS GIVEN TO ORGANIZATIONS WHICH CARRY OUT THEIR RELIGIOUS OR CHARITABLE ACTIVITIES WITHIN MIAMI-DADE COUNTY, FLORIDA AND GRANTS ARE MADE TO OTHER ORGANIZATIONS ONLY IN UNUSUAL CIRCUMSTANCES.
