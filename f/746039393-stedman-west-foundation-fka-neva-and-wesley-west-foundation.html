How to apply

ContactSTEDMAN WEST FOUNDATION ATTN STUART
AddressPO BOX 7, HOUSTON, TX, 77001
Phone7135200400
Form & materialsAPPLICATIONS FOR GRANTS SHOULD BE SUBMITTED IN LETTER OR PROPOSAL FORM. ORGANIZATIONS THAT APPLY SHOULD INCLUDE A BRIEF DESCRIPTION OF THE ORGANIZATION'S ACTIVITY, THE PROPOSED USE OF THE FUNDS REQUESTED,THE AMOUNT OF FUNDS REQUESTED AND OTHER INFORMATION CONSIDERED PERTINENT TO THE REQUEST.
DeadlinesGRANTS MAY BE MADE AT ANY MEETING OF THE TRUSTEES. APPLICATIONS SHOULD BE SUBMITTED PRIOR TO THE FIR
RestrictionsGRANTS ARE MADE ONLY TO PUBLICLY SUPPORTED 501(C)(3) ORGANIZATIONS. APPLICATIONS FROM INDIVIDUALS ARE NOT CONSIDERED. IN AWARDING GRANTS, THE FOUNDATION GIVES PREFERENCE TO TEXAS ORGANIZATIONS. THE MAJORITY OF GRANTS ARE MADE TO MEDICAL, EDUCATIONAL AND CULTURAL ARTS ORGANIZATIONS; HOWEVER, TO THE EXTENT THAT FUNDS ARE AVAILABLE, GRANTS MAY BE MADE FOR OTHER PURPOSES AND TO ORGANIZATIONS IN OTHER STATES.
