RestrictionsGRANTS ARE AWARDED TO PROGRAMS SUPPORTING ARTS AND ENVIRONMENTAL EDUCATION WITHIN THE GREATER CINCINNATI, LOS ANGELES COUNTY, BOSTON, AND MID-COAST MAINE AREAS. APPLICANTS MUST WAIT THREE YEARS BEFORE SUBMITTING ANOTHER PROPOSAL, REGARDLESS OF FUNDING DECISION.
