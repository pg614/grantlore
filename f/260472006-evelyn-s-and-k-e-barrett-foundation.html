DeadlinesBetween March 1 and March 31 annually
RestrictionsThe Foundation awards grants based on the following Focus Areas specifically related to the support of children:Health and WellnessEducationHuman Services (Food, Water and Shelter)Additionally, the Foundations principal focus is to make grants to benefit children up to the age of 18. The Foundations preference is to support projects ranging from start-up/pilot programs to the renewal of existing programs. The Foundation will also provide grants for general operating support to organizations whose mission is in alignment with the Foundations Focus Areas.All entities applying for grants from the Foundation are required to be qualified as a 501(c)(3) organization. Annual Foundation grants are competitive and not all applications can be approved.The Foundation does not make grants to:IndividualsEndowmentsPolitical campaignsOrganizations that discriminate on the basis of religion, gender, race, ethnicity, disabilities or political beliefs
