RestrictionsALL APPLICANTS MUST HAVE A CURRENT 501(C)(3) DESIGNATION FROM THE IRS. THE PEDIGREE FOUNDATION LIMITS ITS AWARDS TO GRANTS FOR PROJECT THAT BENEFIT DOGS.
